Privacy policy

Your data stays with you.

Last updated October 18, 2026

Zarhart is a flight simulator and FAA written-test study tool made by Sophia Ramalli. This policy covers the Zarhart website (ramalliss.github.io/zarhart) and the Zarhart apps for iOS and Android.

What we don't collect

  • No account is needed, and we don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • No ads, no advertising IDs, and no analytics or tracking.
  • We never see your payment card details.

What stays on your device

Your simulator settings, flashcard progress, quiz and practice-exam scores, and lesson state are saved in your browser's or app's local storage on your device. They aren't sent to us. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

In-app purchases

Purchases in the app are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their terms. To confirm what you've bought and restore it on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and transaction details, and basic device and app information such as platform, OS version, app version and store country. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the web version loads fonts from Google Fonts and the 3D library from jsDelivr. Like any web server, those services may receive your IP address and browser details when pages load. The apps include these files, so they don't load them from the internet.

Children

Zarhart isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/ramalliss/zarhart/issues.
