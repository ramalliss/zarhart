Refund policy

If the Pass Pack isn't what you expected, you can get your money back.

Last updated 19 October 2026

Website purchases: 14-day money-back guarantee

If you bought the Pass Pack on our website and it isn't what you expected, ask for a refund within 14 days of your purchase and we'll refund it in full. You don't need to give a reason.

  • How to ask. Reply to your purchase receipt email, or use the support contact on that receipt, and include your order number.
  • Where the money goes. Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Your bank or card issuer decides how long it takes to show up, usually a few business days.
  • After a refund. Your license to the Pass Pack ends, so please delete your copy.
  • Repeat requests. We may decline repeated refund requests for the same product.

Purchases in the Zarhart app

Purchases in the iPhone, iPad or Android app are processed by Apple or Google, and only they can issue refunds:

  • App Store: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play: request a refund through Google Play. See Google Play's refund help.

To restore something you've already bought on a new device, open the Pass Pack in the app and choose “Restore purchases.”
