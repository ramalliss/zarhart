Practice knowledge test

60 questions, 2 hours 30 minutes, no feedback until you submit. That's the same format as the FAA test at a PSI center. Your report groups misses by topic and ACS area.

  • Questions are drawn from every topic in proportion to the real test: most from regulations and weather, fewer from wake turbulence.
  • Choices are shuffled every time.
  • Jump between questions with the number grid.
  • Scores stay on this device only.

The free path to your written test

  1. 1

    Ground-school lessons

    FLY8MA free ground school: video lessons for every topic. Free · no written-test endorsement

  2. 2

    Study handbook

    FAA Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge. Each Zarhart topic lists its chapter. Free PDF

  3. 3

    Practice exams: start here

    Sporty's free practice tests: unlimited timed 60-question tests. Free account

  4. 4

    More practice questions

    King Schools free FAA test, plus the Zarhart practice exam. Free

  5. 5

    Get your endorsement

    A CFI or a home-study course must sign you off before you test. The free FLY8MA tier doesn't include it. Required

  6. 6

    Get your FAA Tracking Number (FTN)

    Register in FAA IACRA. Your name must match your ID exactly. Free

  7. 7

    Book the official written exam

    Schedule at a testing center through the FAA testing portal (PSI). $175 + tax
