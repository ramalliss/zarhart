2. A study aid, not flight training

  • Zarhart is not FAA-approved training, and time in the simulator can't be logged toward a certificate or rating.
  • Nothing on Zarhart is for real-world navigation or flight operations. The checklists are generic training checklists for the simulator's C172-class trainer. In a real airplane, always use its Pilot's Operating Handbook.
  • Regulations, procedures and test fees change. Check the current 14 CFR, AIM and FAA guidance, and follow your flight instructor.
  • We can't promise you'll pass the knowledge test or any other exam.
