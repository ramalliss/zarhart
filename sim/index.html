VR with your phone

Fly in a phone VR viewer.

  1. Pair a game controller in your phone's Bluetooth settings (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro or MFi). You fly with it, because you can't touch the screen inside the viewer.
  2. Turn your phone sideways, tap Start, then slide it into the viewer with the line down the middle.
  3. Look straight ahead and tap the screen (or press the viewer's button) to recenter. Double-tap to leave.

See two images instead of one? Lower the eye spacing. Everything looks too big or too small? Change the field of view.

No viewer or controller yet? “Try it without a viewer” shows one full-screen view that turns as you move your phone, with the normal on-screen stick and throttle.
