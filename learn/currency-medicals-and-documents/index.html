Currency, medicals and documents

A cluster of numbers the written test loves: how long a medical lasts, how recently you must have landed to carry passengers, which papers must be on board and when the airplane needs inspecting. Here they are in one place, with the regulation for each.

Zarhart study notes · Sources listed below · Updated October 2026

Medical certificates (for private pilot privileges)

Your age at the examAny class of medical is good forRule
Under 4060 calendar months14 CFR 61.23
40 or older24 calendar months14 CFR 61.23

First- and second-class medicals last a shorter time for the commercial and airline privileges that need them, then continue as third-class privileges for the periods above. BasicMed is an alternative for many private pilots: a medical exam with a state-licensed physician every 48 months and an online course every 24 calendar months.

What you carry, and what the airplane carries

You: your pilot certificate, your medical certificate (or BasicMed documents) and government photo ID (14 CFR 61.3).

The airplane: ARROW. Airworthiness certificate (displayed at the cabin entrance), Registration, Radio station license (only for international flights), Operating limitations (the POH or AFM, placards and markings) and Weight and balance data.

Required equipment

Day VFR (ATOMATOFLAMES): Airspeed indicator, Tachometer, Oil pressure gauge, Manifold pressure gauge (altitude engines), Altimeter, Temperature gauge (liquid-cooled engines), Oil temperature gauge, Fuel gauge, Landing gear position indicator, Anticollision lights (aircraft certificated after March 11, 1996), Magnetic compass, ELT and Safety belts. Night adds FLAPS: Fuses (spares), Landing light (if for hire), Anticollision lights, Position lights and a Source of electrical power (14 CFR 91.205).

Practice questions

From the Zarhart question bank, written from FAA references in the style of the knowledge test. Pick an answer, then open it to check.

  1. How long is a third-class medical certificate valid for private pilot privileges if you were under 40 on the date of the exam?

    1. A60 calendar months
    2. B24 calendar months
    3. C12 calendar months
    Show answer

    A. 60 calendar months

    Under 40 on the exam date: 60 calendar months. Age 40 or older: 24 calendar months.

    14 CFR 61.23(d) · Open in Zarhart Prep

  2. To act as pilot in command, a private pilot must have completed a flight review within the preceding

    1. A12 calendar months
    2. B24 calendar months
    3. C36 calendar months
    Show answer

    B. 24 calendar months

    A flight review is at least 1 hour of flight training and 1 hour of ground training, every 24 calendar months.

    14 CFR 61.56 · Open in Zarhart Prep

  3. To carry passengers at night, the three required takeoffs and landings must have been

    1. Atouch-and-goes, between sunset and sunrise
    2. Bto a full stop, between 1 hour after sunset and 1 hour before sunrise
    3. Cto a full stop, during civil twilight
    Show answer

    B. to a full stop, between 1 hour after sunset and 1 hour before sunrise

    Night passenger currency needs three full-stop takeoffs and landings in the night period defined as 1 hour after sunset to 1 hour before sunrise, within 90 days.

    14 CFR 61.57(b) · Open in Zarhart Prep

  4. A transponder must have been tested and inspected within the preceding

    1. A24 calendar months
    2. B12 calendar months
    3. C36 calendar months
    Show answer

    A. 24 calendar months

    Transponders need a test and inspection every 24 calendar months.

    14 CFR 91.413 · Open in Zarhart Prep
