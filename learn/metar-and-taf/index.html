Reading METARs and TAFs

A METAR is what the weather is doing right now at an airport. A TAF is what forecasters expect over the next 24 to 30 hours within 5 statute miles of it. Both use the same compact code, and the written test expects you to read it fluently.

Zarhart study notes · Sources listed below · Updated October 2026

A METAR, group by group

Here is a routine report from Zarhart Field, decoded one group at a time:

Example METARMETAR KZAR 191753Z 27012G18KT 10SM -RA BKN035 OVC060 18/09 A3002 RMK AO2

GroupMeaning
METARA routine hourly report. SPECI marks a special report issued when conditions change significantly between hours.
KZARThe ICAO station identifier. Airports in the contiguous US start with K.
191753ZObserved on the 19th of the month at 1753 UTC (Zulu).
27012G18KTWind from 270° true at 12 knots, gusting to 18. VRB means variable; 00000KT means calm.
10SMPrevailing visibility 10 statute miles.
-RALight rain. A minus sign means light, no sign moderate, a plus sign heavy. VC means in the vicinity (5 to 10 SM).
BKN035 OVC060Broken clouds at 3,500 ft AGL and an overcast layer at 6,000 ft AGL. The ceiling is the lowest broken or overcast layer: 3,500 ft.
18/09Temperature 18 °C, dew point 9 °C. An M in front means minus, as in M04.
A3002Altimeter setting 30.02 inHg.
RMK AO2Remarks follow. AO2 is an automated station that can tell rain from snow (AO1 can't).

METAR and TAF winds are referenced to true north. Winds you hear from ATIS or a tower are magnetic, so they line up with runway numbers.

Sky cover and the ceiling

CodeCoverageNotes
SKC / CLRClearCLR is used by automated stations and means no clouds below 12,000 ft.
FEW1/8 to 2/8Not a ceiling.
SCT3/8 to 4/8Scattered. Not a ceiling.
BKN5/8 to 7/8Broken. The lowest broken layer is a ceiling.
OVC8/8Overcast. A ceiling.
VVSky obscuredVertical visibility into fog, smoke or snow, such as VV005 = 500 ft. Counts as a ceiling.

Cloud heights are in hundreds of feet above the ground. CB (cumulonimbus) or TCU (towering cumulus) after a layer, as in BKN015CB, means thunderstorm clouds.

A TAF and its change groups

Example TAFTAF KZAR 191730Z 1918/2024 26010KT P6SM SCT040
FM192200 30015G25KT 5SM -SHRA BKN025
TEMPO 2000/2003 3SM TSRA BKN015CB
PROB30 2006/2010 1SM BR OVC005

  • 191730Z 1918/2024: issued on the 19th at 1730Z, valid from 1800Z on the 19th to 2400Z on the 20th (30 hours). TAFs are issued four times a day and cover 5 SM around the runway complex.
  • 26010KT P6SM SCT040: the opening forecast. Wind 260° at 10 knots, visibility more than 6 SM, scattered clouds at 4,000 ft.
  • FM192200: from 2200Z on the 19th, a lasting change: wind 300° at 15 gusting 25, 5 SM in light rain showers, broken at 2,500 ft.
  • TEMPO 2000/2003: between 0000Z and 0300Z on the 20th, temporary fluctuations (each under an hour, covering less than half the period) to 3 SM in thunderstorms with rain and a broken cumulonimbus layer at 1,500 ft.
  • PROB30 2006/2010: a 30 percent chance, between 0600Z and 1000Z, of 1 SM in mist with an overcast at 500 ft.
  • BECMG (not in the example) means a gradual change during the stated period.

The test's favorite trapP6SM means more than 6 statute miles. A ceiling needs BKN, OVC or VV, never FEW or SCT.

Practice questions

From the Zarhart question bank, written from FAA references in the style of the knowledge test. Pick an answer, then open it to check.

  1. In a METAR, the wind group 27015G25KT means

    1. Awind from 270° magnetic at 15 knots, gusting to 25 knots
    2. Bwind from 270° true at 15 knots, gusting to 25 knots
    3. Cwind from 270° true at 25 knots, gusting to 15 knots
    Show answer

    B. wind from 270° true at 15 knots, gusting to 25 knots

    METAR winds are true direction and knots. Winds given by ATIS or a tower are magnetic.

    AC 00-45; PHAK ch. 13 · Open in Zarhart Prep

  2. In a METAR, "BKN008 OVC015" means the ceiling is

    1. A1,500 ft AGL
    2. B800 ft AGL
    3. C800 ft MSL
    Show answer

    B. 800 ft AGL

    The ceiling is the lowest broken or overcast layer (or vertical visibility). Heights are hundreds of feet above ground level.

    PHAK ch. 13 · Open in Zarhart Prep

  3. In a METAR, "VV005" means

    1. Avisibility 5 statute miles
    2. Ban indefinite ceiling with vertical visibility of 500 ft
    3. Cvariable visibility of 1/2 mile
    Show answer

    B. an indefinite ceiling with vertical visibility of 500 ft

    VV is vertical visibility into an obscuration, such as fog. It counts as a ceiling.

    PHAK ch. 13 · Open in Zarhart Prep

  4. In a TAF, "TEMPO 1520/1523 1SM BR" means

    1. Aa permanent change to 1 SM in mist at 2000Z
    2. Btemporary fluctuations to 1 SM in mist, each lasting less than an hour, between 2000Z and 2300Z on the 15th
    3. Ca 40 percent chance of mist
    Show answer

    B. temporary fluctuations to 1 SM in mist, each lasting less than an hour, between 2000Z and 2300Z on the 15th

    TEMPO is temporary fluctuations of less than an hour, covering less than half the period.

    PHAK ch. 13 · Open in Zarhart Prep
