Questions

Before you buy.

Is the simulator really free?

Yes. Flying, all 13 lessons, the 175 practice questions, flashcards, quizzes and the practice exam are free and need no account. The paid plans add printable study material and future content; they don't lock anything that's free today.

What's the difference between the Pass Pack and Pro?

The Pass Pack is a one-time purchase of printable reference cards, checklists, an exam-day plan and flashcards. Pro is a subscription that includes the Pass Pack and adds new scenario packs, adaptive practice and more scenery as they're released.

Where can I buy?

Purchases are coming to the Zarhart app for iPhone and Android first, through the App Store and Google Play. Buying on the website is coming soon. Until a plan shows a buy button, it isn't on sale yet.

Is Zarhart FAA-approved?

No. Zarhart is a study aid. It isn't FAA-approved training, and time in it can't be logged. You still need an endorsement from a flight instructor or a home-study course before you take the knowledge test.

How do refunds work?

Pass Pack purchases on the website come with a 14-day money-back guarantee: reply to your receipt and you get a full refund, no reason needed. Purchases in the app are refunded by Apple or Google under their policies. See the refund policy.

What do you do with my data?

Your progress stays on your device. Read the privacy policy for the details.
